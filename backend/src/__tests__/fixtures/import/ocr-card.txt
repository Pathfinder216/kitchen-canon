Grandma's Oatmeal Cookies

Serves 24
Total time: 35 minutes

Ingredients
1 cup butter, softened
3/4 cup brown sugar
2 eggs
1 1/2 cups flour
3 cups rolled oats
1 tsp cinnamon

Directions
1. Heat oven to 350F.
2. Cream the butter and sugar, then beat in the eggs.
3. Stir in the flour, cinnamon and oats.
4. Drop by spoonfuls onto a baking sheet and bake 10 to 12 minutes.
